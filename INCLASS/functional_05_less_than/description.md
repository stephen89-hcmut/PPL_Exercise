# Bai 5 - Functional Programming: lessThan

Viet ba ham `lessThan(n, lst)` tra ve cac phan tu nho hon `n` theo ba cach:

- `lessThan_lst_comp`: list comprehension.
- `lessThan_recursive`: de quy.
- `lessThan_higher_order`: higher-order function.

Dieu kien la `<`, khong phai `<=`. Vi du `lessThan(50, [1, 55, 6, 2])` cho `[1, 6, 2]`.
