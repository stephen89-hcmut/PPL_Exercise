# Bai 3 - Functional Programming: double

Viet ba ham tra ve danh sach moi, trong do moi phan tu bang hai lan phan tu tuong ung cua `lst`:

- `double_lst_comp(lst)`: list comprehension.
- `double_recursive(lst)`: de quy.
- `double_higher_order(lst)`: higher-order function.

Vi du: `double([5, 7, 12, -4])` cho `[10, 14, 24, -8]`. Sinh vien implement trong `solution.py`.
